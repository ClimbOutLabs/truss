Test with a saved photo

Sends a saved photo just as the glasses would, and it counts as a real sighting: a question it answers stays answered for today. So the test is off while a question is being watched where a wrong answer would do harm, or where the question is whether something was done.

Truss writes the steps, speaks them one at a time in the glasses, and moves on when a photo shows a step is done.
